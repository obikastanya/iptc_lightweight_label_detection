# Post-hoc recheck of the label-audit disagreements (sensitivity analysis)

Generated by `scripts/label_audit/recheck.py`. Official audit decisions are final and unchanged; this is an unblinded reread of the 62 disagree items whose final status is `wrong` (55) or `both_acceptable` (7). Each article was reread in its original language with the section name, the mapped label, Annotator A, the adjudicator and the human decision visible. Item-level judgments: `recheck_disagreements.csv`.

Mapping table: `src/statickd/testset/iptc_reference/mapping_table.csv` (exported by `src/statickd/testset/iptc_map.py`; byte-identical to `_archive/code_v1/testset_jsonld/iptc_reference/mapping_table.csv`). Re-running `map_section` / `iptc_label` on the stored section names reproduces the label of all 8,650 audit-population articles (6,050 CC-NEWS, 2,600 external), so this is the table the unified test set was built with.

`recheck_status` is the status the editorial label would get under this reread (correct / both_acceptable / wrong). Concurring with an original `both_acceptable` verdict is recorded as `section_acceptable` with `recheck_status = both_acceptable` (no change).

## 2. Mapping errors

None. In every one of the 62 items the section name itself is mapped to the IPTC topic it names (e.g. Religious -> religion, Crime -> crime, Fashion -> arts > fashion, Celebrities/明星/Famosos -> human interest > celebrity, Pogoda -> weather, Наука и технологии -> science and technology). The mismatches come from where the newsroom or dataset filed the article, not from the mapping table, so no mapping rule has to be propagated to other test articles.

Observations:

- Celebrity sections (human interest > celebrity; 251 audit-population articles, all CC-NEWS): 9 of the 12 sampled human-interest items were disagreements. Annotator A labelled most celebrity stories `arts`. On reread, A032 is human interest, A073/A189 are both, A101 both (unchanged); A050 and A269 are music news filed under a celebrity section. The mapping follows IPTC; the gap is an annotator tendency plus K-pop/entertainment news in star sections.
- Fashion (arts > fashion): A145 and A181 are fashion/footwear articles that the LLM chain put under `lifestyle`, which has no fashion node in IPTC; the editorial label was right. A121 (lampshades, Marathi 'Fashion') is a real misfiling.
- L3Cube Tamil categories are noisy: 4 of 10 sampled Tamil items are wrong (Politics: cricket birthday, okra prices; Cinema: G20 summit, POCSO bail). This is dataset category noise (50 test articles per category), not mapping.
- Section names that are fine but carry off-topic articles: IndoNews/Liputan6 `bisnis` with job vacancies and civil-service recruitment (A098, A119, A191; regex count of recruitment terms in the 50 `bisnis` test articles: 6), finanzen.net `Aktien` with general dpa-AFX wire news (A176), L3Cube `education`/`Crime`/`sports` with exam notices, accidents and off-field stories.

## 3. Sensitivity of the estimated editorial-label accuracy

Same population-weighted estimator and stratified bootstrap (2,000 resamples, same seed) as `scripts/label_audit/analyze.py` (functions imported, script not edited). Only the status of the 62 rechecked items changes; spot-check error e stays 0.

| scenario | strict | 95% CI | lenient | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| official (final decisions) | 79.3% | 74.4-83.9% | 81.9% | 77.1-86.3% |
| recheck (item-level recheck_status) | 80.4% | 75.7-84.8% | 86.1% | 82.1-90.1% |
| upper bound: every section_acceptable and annotation_error item counted correct | 86.1% | 82.1-90.1% | 86.1% | 82.1-90.1% |

Per stratum (recheck):

| stratum | official strict / lenient | recheck strict / lenient |
|---|---|---|
| ccnews_jsonld | 82.0% / 84.0% | 83.0% / 89.5% |
| l3cube_indic | 72.9% / 77.1% | 74.3% / 78.6% |
| bangla_plos | 73.3% / 73.3% | 73.3% / 73.3% |
| indonews | 80.0% / 80.0% | 80.0% / 80.0% |

The recheck row is the realistic sensitivity: strict moves only through the 3 items that become correct; lenient additionally through the `wrong` items that become both_acceptable. The upper-bound row also turns all both_acceptable-type items (including the 7 original ones) into strict correct, which overstates accuracy because those articles have a second, equally valid topic. The unclear item (A027) keeps its official status in every row.
